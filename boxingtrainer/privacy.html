DRILLIO — BOXING TRAINER

Privacy
Policy.

Effective date: November 15, 2026

Drillio — Boxing Trainer (the “app”) is an offline-first training companion for combat-sports bag work and shadowboxing. Spoken audio coaching is in development. The app works without an account, and your training profile and workout records are stored on your device.

Developer and data controller: Bartosz Jarmuż, publishing as Cardio Can Wait (CardioCanWait). Contact: cardiocanwait@gmail.com. “We” means the developer. This policy is also available in Simplified Chinese. If the versions differ, the English version applies generally; for users in mainland China, the Simplified Chinese version applies.

1. Scope and app versions

This policy covers the app for iOS and Android, including versions distributed through Apple App Store, Google Play, Samsung Galaxy Store, Huawei AppGallery and other authorized channels. A store or platform version may use different service providers:

  • iOS, Google Play and Samsung Galaxy Store builds may use Google Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics and Remote Config, Google AdMob, Google’s consent form and Apple’s tracking permission where applicable.
  • Huawei AppGallery builds use HUAWEI Ads (Petal Ads) and Huawei Remote Configuration. Optional crash and usage reporting is provided by Sentry when you enable it. These builds do not use the Google services listed above.

Services described for one build do not necessarily operate in another. The app may receive remote configuration values, such as advertising settings, from the provider used by that build.

2. Information you provide and information on your device

The app does not require an account and does not ask you for your name, email address or a profile tied to your identity. Your training mode, workout preferences and settings, generated or downloaded workouts, favourites, workout history and feedback are kept in the app’s private storage on your device. The app does not send these training records or your workout content to us or to an app-specific cloud account.

Workout generation, recommendations and playback work on your device. Local records are removed when you uninstall the app, subject to device operating-system backup behavior.

3. Network requests and curated workouts

When the app launches and an internet connection is available, it checks our static catalog at boxingtrainer-data.pages.dev for curated workouts and downloads new or changed workout files. This catalog check runs automatically; core workout generation remains available offline. It does not upload your generated workouts, profile, history or favourites. Previously downloaded curated workouts remain available offline. When your device makes a request, the catalog host and its infrastructure may receive your IP address, request time and standard technical request information to deliver and secure the files.

On supported builds the app also contacts its platform provider for remote configuration, app-integrity checks, consent handling, advertising, and (when permitted) analytics or crash reporting. Those requests can expose IP address, app and device information, identifiers, language or region, and request diagnostics to the relevant provider. Details depend on the service and your settings; see Third-party providers.

4. Analytics and crash reports

On iOS, Google Play and Samsung builds that include Google telemetry, Firebase Analytics can receive events about app use, such as screens and feature interactions, along with app version, language, store/build flavor and technical device or installation information. Firebase Crashlytics can receive diagnostic details such as crash traces, app and operating-system versions, device model, timestamps, exception messages and technical logs. The app does not intentionally include your workout text, name or email in analytics events. Technical logs and crash details can contain unexpected information, so we cannot guarantee that every report is free of personal information.

Where Google’s consent requirements apply, the app uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) form to collect and update your choices. Analytics and crash uploads are enabled only when the consent result allows them. In other regions, analytics and crash reporting are enabled unless you opt out in the app’s Privacy settings, subject to applicable law. On iOS, Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt separately controls cross-app tracking for advertising; declining it does not prevent use of the app. You can revisit available choices through Privacy settings.

On Huawei AppGallery builds, analytics and crash reporting are not enabled unless you opt in. If enabled, Sentry receives diagnostic reports and limited usage events such as app screens or actions, app version, device and operating-system information, timestamps and technical error details. Sentry collection is optional, and you can change your choice later in Privacy settings. Reports already received may remain for the provider’s retention period.

5. Advertising and your choices

The app is supported by ads. Depending on the build, ads are served by Google AdMob or HUAWEI Ads. These providers may process advertising identifiers (for example, Android advertising IDs, Apple’s identifier when authorized, or Huawei’s OAID), IP address, device and app information, approximate region, ad interactions and diagnostics to deliver, measure and protect ads. Their processing is governed by their own privacy terms.

Google’s UMP form collects advertising choices where required. On iOS, cross-app tracking also depends on Apple’s tracking permission and your system settings. Huawei AppGallery builds show a first-launch privacy notice with separate choices for ad personalization and optional diagnostics. Ads may still be shown when you decline personalization; the app then requests non-personalized ads where supported. You can change these choices from the app’s Privacy settings and, where available, your device’s advertising settings. Your choices are saved on your device.

6. Third-party providers

  • Google Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config and App Check — app usage, crash diagnostics, remote settings and app-integrity services on Google-enabled builds. See Firebase privacy and security and Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google AdMob and Google UMP — advertising and consent management on supported builds. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.
  • HUAWEI Ads / Petal Ads and Huawei Remote Configuration — advertising, ad settings and related services on Huawei AppGallery builds. Huawei Ads may use OAID and technical or network information according to its own terms. See Huawei’s Ads Kit guidance and its Privacy Statement.
  • Sentry (EU/Germany) — optional crash and usage reporting on Huawei builds, only when you opt in. See Sentry Privacy Policy.
  • Cloudflare Pages — hosts the supplemental curated workout catalog. Its infrastructure processes ordinary web request information when the app downloads catalog files. See Cloudflare Privacy Policy.
  • Apple, Google, Samsung and Huawei — operate the devices, operating systems, app stores, consent interfaces and, if you choose to rate the app, the store review flow. Their own policies apply to information they receive.

We do not operate an account system, advertising profile database or application-specific server for your training data. Providers may process information under their own terms, including in countries other than where you live.

7. This website

This product page and policy are static pages. They do not use advertising or analytics scripts, set cookies or collect information through a form. The text and fonts are served from this site, so visiting these pages does not request fonts from Google. The website host and its infrastructure may process your IP address, browser details, request time and other standard server logs to deliver and protect the pages.

8. Retention and deletion

Training preferences and records remain on your device until you delete them in the app (where that option exists), clear the app’s storage or uninstall it. Catalog files remain cached locally until removed by the app or you clear its storage. We do not receive those records and therefore cannot delete them remotely.

Firebase Crashlytics states that crash traces and associated identifiers are retained for 90 days before removal begins. Firebase Analytics retention is configured in the project; Google offers user and event data retention settings of up to 14 months. Sentry retains reports according to the account’s configuration and its terms. Google AdMob, HUAWEI Ads, remote configuration, catalog hosting, app stores and other providers retain request or service data under their own policies and settings. Contact us if you have a question about a request we can identify.

9. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction or deletion of personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, to withdraw consent, or to complain to a data-protection authority. Contact us at the address below. Because the app has no accounts and we do not receive your locally stored workout records, we may be unable to connect a provider-side identifier or request to you. You can also contact the relevant service provider directly.

We do not sell your personal information for money. Some privacy laws may treat personalized advertising as targeted advertising or sharing; you can refuse personalization in the app and use device-level advertising controls where available.

10. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, or the higher minimum age required in your country. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us.

11. Security and international transfers

Local workout data is held in the app’s private device storage. Network requests use encrypted HTTPS where supported by the service. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. The providers listed above may process information in other countries under their own privacy notices and transfer safeguards.

12. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as app features, service providers or legal requirements change. We will revise the effective date above and update the policy before materially different data uses begin.

For privacy questions or requests, email cardiocanwait@gmail.com.
